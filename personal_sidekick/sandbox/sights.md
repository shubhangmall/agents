# Popular Tourist Attraction in Lisbon

## Alfama
- **Address:** Mouraria - Castelo - Alfama, 1100 Lisbon, Portugal  
- **Reviews:** Rated 4.4 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with over 21,449 reviews. Visitors appreciate the beauty of the narrow alleys, the history embedded in the architecture, and the lively atmosphere enriched by traditional Fado music. Many reviews highlight the stunning views from the viewpoints (miradouros) within the district. 
  
- **Summary of Reviews:** Visitors frequently mention the charm and authenticity of Alfama, noting its quaint cafes, local shops, and vibrant community. While some express that navigating the steep streets can be challenging, the overall experience is described as enchanting and rewarding, making it a must-visit for anyone exploring Lisbon.

- **Key Attraction Points:**  
  - **Fado Music:** Alfama is renowned for its Fado houses where visitors can experience Portugal's soulful music genre.  
  - **Miradouro de Santa Luzia:** A picturesque viewpoint that offers stunning vistas over the Tagus River and the rooftops of Alfama.  
  - **São Miguel Church:** A beautiful church showcasing traditional Portuguese architecture, often visited for its serene atmosphere.  
  - **Alfama's Streets:** Meandering through the labyrinth of narrow cobbled streets where one can stumble upon hidden gems, quaint shops, and local eateries.
